I am a PhD candidate in bioinformatics at ShanghaiTech University, working on cancer multi-omics, with a focus on the mechanisms of metastasis and recurrence in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). By integrating genomics, transcriptomics, and proteomics data from primary and recurrent tumors, I aim to characterize the evolutionary dynamics of cancer cells during metastatic progression and to identify factors driving post-operative recurrence. I am also involved in translational research on esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC).

Before starting my PhD, I received my B.Eng. in bioengineering from Henan University of Technology (2018–2022).

I am dedicated to developing and applying data-driven methods to discover novel biomarkers and therapeutic targets, and to building computational resources that make large-scale cancer omics data easier to interpret and reuse.

## Research Interests

- **Mechanisms of Cancer Metastasis and Recurrence**: Investigating the molecular and cellular processes driving cancer metastasis and post-curative-treatment recurrence, with a focus on liver cancer, to identify potential therapeutic targets.

- **Single-cell, Multi-omics and Machine Learning**: Integrating genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and single-cell multi-omics data, and applying machine learning and foundation models — including single-cell, spatial and pathology models — to cell-state representation, tissue phenotyping, and cross-modal analysis of tumor biology and its clinical implications.

- **Biomarker Discovery and Translational Research**: Identifying novel biomarkers for prognosis and recurrence prediction through multi-omics data and machine-learning based survival modelling, and supporting the development of personalized treatment strategies for liver cancer and other malignancies.
