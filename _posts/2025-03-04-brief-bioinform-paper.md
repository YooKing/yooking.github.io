Our benchmarking study "Benchmarking copy number aberrations inference tools using single-cell multi-omics datasets" is published in *Briefings in Bioinformatics*. We systematically evaluated five popular tools (inferCNV, CopyKAT, SCEVAN, Numbat, CaSpER) for inferring copy number aberrations from scRNA-seq data, and provide practical guidelines for method selection. [Link](https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbaf076)
