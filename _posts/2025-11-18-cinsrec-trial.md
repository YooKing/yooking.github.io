Our clinical study "Neoadjuvant chemoimmunotherapy combined with node-sparing radiotherapy for clinical T3N+ locally advanced esophageal squamous cell carcinoma: a prospective single-arm, phase II study (CINSREC trial)" is published in *Thoracic Cancer*. The trial evaluated tislelizumab plus chemotherapy combined with node-sparing radiotherapy before surgery in ESCC patients. [Link](https://doi.org/10.1111/1759-7714.70191)
